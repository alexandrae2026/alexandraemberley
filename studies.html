Embroidery and Tailoring Studies

École Lesage, Paris, and the Tailoring Academy, Macclesfield

Couture embroidery, École Lesage

2016–2018

I studied at the École Lesage, Paris, in three stays: Introduction to the Lunéville Hook and Traditional Stitches in the summer of 2016; the professional certification in couture embroidery from September to December 2017, generously funded by a grant from the Alberta Foundation for the Arts; and the goldwork study in the spring of 2018.

Woman’s Bespoke Jacket

September 2023 – February 2024

After completing my MFA in Textiles at RISD, I studied at the Tailoring Academy in Macclesfield from September 2023 to February 2024. Working from individual measurements, I drafted, cut, fitted, and constructed a women's bespoke jacket under instruction. The program covered the combined roles of cutter and tailor, including cloth planning, internal canvas and support, baste and forward fittings, collar and sleeve setting, lining, hand-finishing, quality control, and production documentation.

From draft to first form

The jacket was developed from an individual cutting pattern based on body measurements and figure analysis. The cloth layout was planned to use the tweed efficiently while maintaining grain, balance, and matching across the garment. After the component pieces were cut and marked, the fronts, back, canvas, pockets, and supporting layers were assembled in preparation for the first baste fitting.

After the baste fitting: alterations and pockets

Amendments from the fitting were marked, the body opened and adjusted, and the pattern corrected before the committed handwork began.
